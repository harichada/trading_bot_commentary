## WHAT "WINNING STRATEGY" MEANS

When we describe a strategy as "winning" or "profitable," we mean:

- It showed positive returns in **backtesting** on historical data
- Backtesting includes realistic assumptions (slippage, borrow costs, fill simulation)
- It has been validated through walk-forward optimization on out-of-sample data

**It does NOT mean:**

- It will be profitable in the future
- It will be profitable every month
- It will be profitable for your specific account size
- It has been verified by any regulatory body
