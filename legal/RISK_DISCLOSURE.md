## KEY RISKS

### 1. YOU MAY LOSE MONEY

Algorithmic trading can result in significant financial losses. There is no trading strategy that guarantees profits. The Rudra Trading Engine may execute trades that result in partial or total loss of your invested capital.

### 2. PAST PERFORMANCE DOES NOT PREDICT FUTURE RESULTS

Any backtested results, historical performance data, or simulated returns shown on the platform are **hypothetical**. They were achieved with the benefit of hindsight using historical data and do not account for:

- Real-time execution delays and slippage
- Market impact of actual order placement
- Broker fees, commissions, and borrowing costs not fully modeled
- Changing market conditions and regimes
- Events not present in historical data (pandemics, wars, flash crashes)

**A strategy that returned +1,465% in backtesting may lose money in live trading.**

### 3. AUTOMATED TRADING SPECIFIC RISKS

- **Software failures**: Bugs, crashes, or connectivity loss may prevent the system from executing trades, closing positions, or enforcing stop losses
- **Stop-loss gaps**: In fast-moving markets, stop-loss orders may execute at prices significantly worse than the stop price
- **API failures**: Your broker's API may become unavailable, preventing order execution or position management
- **Data errors**: Incorrect market data may cause the system to make inappropriate trading decisions
- **Over-optimization**: Strategies optimized on historical data may not perform similarly in live markets (curve-fitting risk)

### 4. SHORT SELLING RISKS

The Service may execute short sales, which carry theoretically unlimited loss potential. A shorted stock can rise indefinitely, and short squeezes can cause rapid, extreme price increases.

### 5. MARKET RISKS

- **Volatility**: Markets can experience extreme volatility that exceeds the parameters of any trading strategy
- **Liquidity**: Some stocks may become illiquid, making it difficult to exit positions at desired prices
- **Trading halts**: Exchanges may halt trading in individual stocks or market-wide, trapping open positions
- **Gap risk**: Overnight or intraday gaps can bypass stop-loss orders entirely
- **Regulatory changes**: New regulations could impact the viability of certain trading strategies

### 6. TECHNOLOGY RISKS

- **Internet connectivity**: Loss of internet connection between the Service and your broker prevents trade management
- **Server downtime**: Our servers may experience planned or unplanned downtime
- **Third-party dependencies**: We rely on third-party services (brokers, data providers, cloud infrastructure) that may fail
